Find Your Partner ❤️
Find Your Partner is a fun, interactive camera filter website inspired by Snapchat. It uses your live camera and randomly reveals details about your future partner, such as name, age, height, personality, hobby, and profession.
Features
- Live camera access.
- Random partner predictions.
- Animated filter effects.
- Capture and download photos.
- Try again for new predictions.
- Mobile and desktop support.
